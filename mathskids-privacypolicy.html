Privacy Policy for MathsKids

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

MathsKids ("the App") is an educational game designed for children and families. We are committed to protecting the privacy and safety of our users and complying with applicable child privacy requirements and Google Play Families Policy requirements.

1. Information We Collect

MathsKids does not require users to create an account or build a personal profile. We do not directly ask you for information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Home Address
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Contacts
  • Precise Location Information

Separately from the above, the third-party advertising services described in Section 3 collect and share certain technical information — including an IP-derived approximate location, app interaction data, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers — as part of serving and measuring advertisements. This technical information is distinct from the personal information listed above, which the App itself never requests.

2. Children's Privacy

MathsKids is intended for children and families.

MathsKids does not ask children to provide their names, contact details, photographs, or other personal profile information. However, the third-party advertising services described in Section 3 may collect and process limited technical information, including device identifiers and IP-derived approximate location, under the App's child-directed advertising configuration.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

The App is configured to request advertising as child-directed and non-personalized. This configuration governs how the App itself requests ads; it is not an independent guarantee of how every advertising partner or ad network ultimately handles a given request. See Section 3 for further detail on the advertising services involved.

3. Advertising

MathsKids may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob is integrated with two additional mediation technologies that it can ask to supply an ad in its place: Unity Ads and Kidoz. As of this policy's last update, both the Unity Ads and Kidoz mediation integrations are paused, so ads are currently served through Google AdMob directly.

Ad requests are sent as non-personalized and child-directed (see Section 2). The App does not request the Android AD_ID permission and configures advertising requests for child-directed treatment. Under Google's documented child-directed advertising behavior, the Android Advertising ID is not transmitted with those requests. Other technical identifiers and information may still be processed as described below. Google AdMob, and Unity Ads and Kidoz if their mediation is reactivated, may process the following:

  • Approximate location, derived from IP address
  • App interaction data, such as how and when ads are requested and displayed
  • Diagnostics, such as technical and performance data related to serving ads
  • Device or other identifiers, other than AD_ID, which is excluded as noted above

This information is collected and shared with these advertising partners for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes — for example, to serve and measure ads, detect invalid traffic, and maintain service functionality.

For more information regarding these providers' privacy practices, please visit:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Kidoz: https://www.kidoz.net/privacy-policies

4. In-App Purchases

MathsKids may offer optional in-app purchases.

All purchases are processed securely through Google Play. MathsKids does not collect, receive, or store payment card information.

5. Local Device Storage

The App stores certain information locally on the user's device in order to provide game functionality, including:

  • Game Progress
  • Earned Stars
  • Achievements
  • Selected Cat Character
  • Audio Preferences
  • Gameplay Preferences
  • Unlocked Content
  • App Settings

This gameplay progress and these settings remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to us as gameplay records. Separate technical information handled by third-party advertising services is described in Section 3.

6. Third-Party Services

MathsKids may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads (advertising mediation, currently paused — see Section 3)
  • Kidoz (advertising mediation, currently paused — see Section 3)

These services operate under their own privacy policies and may process limited technical information necessary for their operation. See:

  • Google (Play Services, Play Billing, AdMob): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Kidoz: https://www.kidoz.net/privacy-policies

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information used by the App and maintain a safe experience for users.

8. Data Deletion

Users may manage locally stored game data as follows:

  • The in-app Reset Progress feature removes locally stored gameplay progress, such as stars, achievements, and content unlocked through play. It does not remove purchased entitlements or app settings unrelated to gameplay progress, which remain in effect after a reset.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored app data from the device, subject to Android's own backup and restore behavior, which may retain a copy of some app data outside the App's control.

Neither Reset Progress nor uninstalling the App necessarily deletes information already collected or retained by the third-party advertising providers described in Section 3; requests regarding that information should be directed to those providers.

9. Parents and Guardians

Parents and guardians may contact us regarding questions about privacy practices or requests relating to information stored by the App.

10. Compliance

MathsKids is designed to comply with applicable Google Play Families Policy requirements and child privacy obligations.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with an updated revision date.
