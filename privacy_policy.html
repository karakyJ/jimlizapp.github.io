Privacy Policy

Truth or Dare Kids — Last updated: October 26, 2026

Truth or Dare Kids ("the App") is a family party game designed for children and families, developed by JimLizApp. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices. The advertising networks, advertising-identifier protections, and purchase-processing details described below are specific to the Android version; this policy does not assert that the same integrations are active on iOS.

No Accounts, No Login

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to play the game.

Data Stored On Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Player names and chosen avatars you enter (never sent anywhere — stays on your device)
  • Which packs you've unlocked and your purchase/rewarded-ad progress
  • Sound and app preference settings

This information is never transmitted to us or any third party. Local gameplay data can also be cleared from the Parent Zone, or by uninstalling the App.

Advertising

On Android, the App shows ads to support free play, served through:

  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads, via Google's mediation (AdMob may ask Unity Ads to supply an ad)
  • Kidoz, a child-safety-certified advertising network for kids' apps (AdMob may ask Kidoz to supply an ad)

On Android, all ad requests are sent as non-personalized and child-directed. Specifically: the App does not transmit the Android device's advertising identifier ("advertising ID," used elsewhere for cross-app ad tracking), and ads are not personalized based on your activity. Separately from the advertising identifier, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and Kidoz may still process other limited technical information — such as your general device information and IP address — to deliver and measure even a non-personalized ad; this is standard baseline ad-serving behavior for these networks, distinct from the advertising-identifier tracking the App specifically avoids. Kidoz states that it is reviewed annually and certified COPPA-compliant under the PRIVO Safe Harbor program. See:

  • Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity's Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Kidoz's Privacy Policies: https://www.kidoz.net/privacy-policies

In-App Purchases

On Android, the App offers optional one-time purchases to unlock bonus content packs and an Ultra Premium upgrade. These purchases are processed entirely by the Google Play Store — we do not receive or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, which is used to unlock the relevant content on your device.

Parental Controls

The App includes a "Parent Zone" protected by a simple math problem, designed to prevent young children from accidentally making purchases or changing settings without a parent or guardian present. A parent gate also protects every purchase and purchase-restoration action in the App.

No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking

The App does not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or behavioral tracking SDKs beyond the non-personalized advertising described above. The App does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Children's Privacy

This App is designed for children. Its Google Play target audience is children ages 5 and under, 6–8, and 9–12 only; no older age group is declared. The specific protections we've put in place for children's privacy are: no account or sign-in is required or offered; advertising is requested as non-personalized and child-directed, and the App does not transmit the device advertising identifier; the App uses no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or behavioral-tracking SDKs; and purchases and data-affecting settings sit behind a Parent Zone math-problem gate. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what's described in this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
